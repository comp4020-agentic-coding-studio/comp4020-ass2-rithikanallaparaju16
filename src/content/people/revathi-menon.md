Teaches vipassana-style insight meditation in the Indian tradition. She is in
this course for one class,
[Ten minutes, guided](/sessions/03-ten-minutes-guided/), after which the
ten-minute opening stops being something the teaching staff improvise.

Invited partly to correct the record. The practice this course opens every
class with was not invented by the researchers who measured it. It was adapted
from Buddhist contemplative traditions and then renamed, and the adaptation is
what most people encounter. Having someone here who works in the source
tradition is the difference between using a technique and knowing where it
came from.

Also unusually unbothered by sceptical questions, which this room produces in
volume. She will tell you when the evidence for something is thin rather than
talking around it, including about her own practice.
