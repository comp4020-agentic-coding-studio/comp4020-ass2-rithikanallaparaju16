Three lectures across the semester, not twelve. They exist to give you the
real science behind what the studios have you doing — and, just as often, to
mark the point where the popular version of that science runs ahead of the
evidence. Any claim on these pages with a name and a year attached is a paper
someone here has read. Anything without one is a hunch, and is labelled as a
hunch.

All three are held **outdoors**, in a spot posted here by the Friday before.
You can suggest venues. Because there is nowhere to wait around, they start on
time and cannot start late.

Each one opens with the same ten minutes of guided attention that every studio
opens with — see [Nothing here is that serious](/lectures/01-not-that-serious/)
for what that is and why it has a slightly silly name.
