The [lectures](/lectures/) and [crits](/sessions/) are listed by week, which is
the right order to *do* the course in and the wrong order to look anything up
in. This page is the same material as **four decks**, grouped by subject.

Each deck is the short version. The claim, the study under it, and the place
the evidence stops. Use them to revise, to find the thing you half-remember,
or to hand somebody the argument in five minutes. The pages behind each deck
carry the full brief.

## [Catching yourself](/decks/catching-yourself/)

The symptom, and the first four weeks. No framework, just the thing you
already recognise.

Rumination versus reflection, why worry is hard to put down, and the exits
people actually use.

*Behind it.* [The layered "OK"](/sessions/01-the-layered-ok/) ·
[The reveal](/sessions/02-the-reveal/) ·
[Why the spiral feels like work](/lectures/02-the-spiral/) ·
[How you stopped](/sessions/06-how-you-stopped/)

## [Why a loop repeats](/decks/why-a-loop-repeats/)

The course's central claim and the evidence under it. Incubation during
undemanding tasks, attention residue, and the phone break that measures like
no break at all.

Includes the three things the course refuses to claim, and the point at which
the argument becomes a hunch.

*Behind it.* [The empty slot](/lectures/05-the-empty-slot/) ·
[The blank that isn't](/lectures/06-the-blank-that-isnt/) ·
[When it helps](/sessions/11-when-it-helps/)

## [Making room](/decks/making-room/)

Building the conditions where a thought can finish. Ten minutes, half speed,
a sandwich in silence, plus what actually helps under load.

Carries the two most uncomfortable findings on the syllabus. Meditation's
adverse-effect rates, and the study saying walk *faster*.

## Still open

Not a topic so much as an admission. Some weeks are deliberately unbooked.
After [the anonymous thread](/assessments/the-anonymous-thread/) the cohort
votes on which student-reported techniques the class tries, and those become
crits in the back half of semester. You can also propose a lecture topic on
the forum and have it voted up.

So there will be a fifth deck, and what goes in it is not up to the teaching
staff. Which is either fitting for a course called Unfinished Thinking or an
excuse. Both, probably.
