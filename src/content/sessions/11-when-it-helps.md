## Before the session

Think of a decision — yours, or one you watched happen — where spending more
time thinking really did produce a better outcome, not just a longer one.

## In the session

Two threads, run one after the other. First, build a shared list of the
group's "worth it" examples and try to find what they have in common — the
stakes, how reversible the decision was, information that genuinely was not
available yet. Second, take one of the spirals from an earlier crit and
imagine it happening across a group instead of in one head: a thread where
everyone's uncertainty feeds everyone else's. What changes?

## Afterwards

This is the last crit before the final project, and the two threads above
are close to that brief — bring them.
