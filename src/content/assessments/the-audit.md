## The brief

> Catch yourself doing it, and say honestly where you could have stopped.

The studios up to this point gave you the tools: naming the layer past
useful, guessing-then-revealing the gap between the story and the outcome,
and running the same machine on a good outcome instead of a bad one. This
assessment asks you to turn those tools on your own week, not on an exercise.

The log does not need drama. The moments that teach the most are often the
smallest — the ones where, in hindsight, you can see exactly where more
thinking stopped being useful and started being something else.

## What you submit

A short document — the log plus the one unpacked moment — in whatever plain
format you like: markdown, a doc, even a photographed notebook page, as long
as it is legible. Say which moment is the unpacked one.
