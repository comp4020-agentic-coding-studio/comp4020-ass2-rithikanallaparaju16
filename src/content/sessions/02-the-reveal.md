## Before the session

Arrive with a small thing you spent way too much energy overthinking. A text,
an outfit, a reply-all, whether a look meant something. Make sure you know how
it actually turned out.

Small is the operative word. Anything you would not want the room laughing at
is the wrong item for today. Picking a light one is not dodging the exercise.
The mechanism is the same at every size, and it is much easier to see when
nothing is at stake.

## In the session

One at a time, someone describes the situation and the spiral, stopping right
before the outcome. The room guesses what happened. Then the reveal. Repeat
until everyone who wants a turn has had one, and "who wants a turn" is meant
literally. Passing costs nothing and needs no reason.

After a few rounds, pause on the pattern rather than the punchline. What did
the guesses tend to get wrong, and in which direction? Watch for the usual
shape, without asserting it up front. Guesses skew toward the version that
was overthought, not the version that actually happened.

## Afterwards

Nothing to submit, but keep this one in mind for your log. The same gap,
between what got built up and what actually happened, is the raw material for
that assessment.
